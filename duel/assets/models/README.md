# Models (optional)

Put a glTF binary at `assets/models/<figure id>.glb` and that figure uses it instead of the placeholder
(sphere on a coloured base). `<figure id>` is the `id` field of the figure in `data/figures.json`
(e.g. `52` for the Pikachu entry). The model is scaled to roughly 0.85 board units tall and placed on the base.

This folder is git-ignored: bring your own assets and respect their licences.
